What is VitaeSMP

LIFESTEAL, DONE RIGHT

VitaeSMP is a Lifesteal SMP built on one idea — the world should be genuinely dangerous. Kill a player and take a heart. Die and lose one. Hit zero and you're out. No hand-holding. No admin interference. Just players, consequence, and the world.

♥

Lifesteal Mechanics

Kill a player and steal their hearts. Die and lose one. The stakes are always real — one bad fight can end your run.

⚖️

No Admin Abuse

No staff flying in with creative mode. Issues go through the ticket system — fair, transparent, no favoritism.

⚡

Zero Lag

Running on a Ryzen 9 7950X with 8GB dedicated RAM and NVMe storage. Performance is not a compromise.

⚔️

Raid & Grief Allowed

It's Lifesteal — build smart, stay paranoid. Alliances, betrayals, and raids are yours to manage.

THE RULES

General Rules

—
Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, threats, or disguised insults.
—
No spam of any kind — messages, macros, emojis, sounds, commands, or bypassing mutes.
—
English only in public chat.
—
Keep content appropriate. No NSFW, gore, offensive memes, or sexual skins/builds.
—
No advertising servers, socials, or links — including indirect or DM advertising.

In-Game Rules

—
No hacks, cheats, or scripts. No autoclickers, x-ray packs, unfair mods, or ghost clients.
—
Allowed mods: OptiFine, Sodium, LabyMod, Lunar — no unfair features enabled.
—
No exploiting. No bugs, glitches, dupes, lag machines, or crash attempts. Report bugs immediately. Benefiting from someone else's exploit still counts.
—
No combat logging. Leaving to avoid death results in a ban.
—
No killing naked or freshly spawned players. Hearts will be refunded unless they started the fight.
—
No heart boosting, kill trading, or staged deaths.
—
No alts for extra hearts, ban evasion, AFK farming, or any advantage. "My sibling's account" is not an excuse.
—
No minimap entity radar, ESP, or player tracking mods.
✔
Griefing and raiding are allowed. It's Lifesteal — build accordingly.

Discord Rules

—
Keep channels on-topic.
—
Don't ping staff unless it's urgent.
—
Use Modmail for help, appeals, and reports. DM appeals are automatically denied.
—
No impersonating staff or other players.
Any attempt to bend, twist, or bypass these rules — even indirectly — will be treated the same as breaking them outright.
These are the summary rules. For full punishment breakdowns and moderator notes, read the complete rules document →

By playing on VitaeSMP or participating in the Discord, you automatically agree to all rules listed here.
