What is this

LIFESTEAL, DONE RIGHT

Shared Survival is a Lifesteal SMP built on one idea: the world should be genuinely dangerous. Kill a player and steal their hearts. Die and lose one. No padding, no safety nets.

♥

Lifesteal Mechanics

Kill a player, gain a heart. Die, lose one. When you hit zero — you're out. The stakes are always real.

⚔

No Active Staff

There's no admin flying in to resolve your problems. Alliances, betrayals, and feuds are yours to handle. Lawless but fair.

⚡

Zero Lag

Running on a Ryzen 9 7950X with 8GB dedicated RAM and NVMe storage. Performance is not a compromise here.

🌍

Shared World

One map. No resets announced without community vote. Build somewhere meaningful — you might just lose it.

Read before joining

THE RULES

01
No Hacking or Cheating

No clients, no exploits, no dupes. Ban is permanent. This is the only rule that gets active enforcement.

02
PvP is Always On

You are never "safe." Do not log out to avoid death — combat logging results in an instant ban.

03
Griefing Spawn is Banned

Everywhere else is fair game. Spawn is neutral ground for new players to get their bearings.

04
No Slurs or Hate Speech

Trash talk is fine. Targeted harassment or slurs are not. Be creative with your insults.

05
Alliances Are Yours to Make or Break

No staff-enforced agreements. Betray whoever you want. The world will remember it though.
